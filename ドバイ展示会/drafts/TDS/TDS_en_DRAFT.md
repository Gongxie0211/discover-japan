## 1. Product Description

Protocare XOP (marketed internationally as **kichi**) is a water-based, penetrating fluoropolymer anti-fouling coating material designed for natural stone and architectural building surfaces. Unlike conventional surface coatings, Protocare XOP penetrates deep into the substrate matrix, providing durable protection against water, oil, and environmental contaminants without forming a surface film.

The product preserves the natural appearance of the substrate — no gloss change, no color shift, no visible coating layer. It is non-flammable and water-based, reducing application hazard and environmental impact compared to solvent-based alternatives.

**Country of Origin:** Japan

## 6. Application Instructions

### Step 1: Surface Preparation

1. Remove all contaminants from the substrate: dust, oil, grease, mold, existing coatings, and mineral deposits.
2. Clean the surface with an appropriate cleaner compatible with the substrate type. Rinse thoroughly and allow to dry completely.
3. Confirm the substrate is fully dry before application. Do not apply to surfaces with elevated moisture content.
4. Check ambient conditions: temperature must be within 5°C–35°C. Do not apply in rain, heavy wind, or when frost or condensation is expected within 24 hours of application.

### Step 2: Application

1. Stir Protocare XOP thoroughly before use.
2. Apply by roller or brush in smooth, even strokes. Avoid pooling or over-application.
3. Apply the first coat at a rate of 0.12–0.18 L/m², adjusted to substrate porosity. Highly porous substrates will absorb more material.
4. Allow the first coat to reach touch-dry state (approximately 2 hours at 20°C) before applying the second coat.
5. Apply the second coat using the same technique and rate as the first.
6. For highly porous or absorbent substrates, a third coat may be applied after the second coat has reached touch-dry state.

### Step 3: Curing and Protection

1. After the final coat, protect the surface from rain, water contact, and foot traffic during the curing period.
2. Full cure at 20°C: allow a minimum of 24 hours. Cooler or more humid conditions will extend cure time.
3. Do not expose the freshly coated surface to rain within the first 24 hours after final application.

## 7. Safety Notes

- Ensure adequate ventilation during application and drying.
- Avoid contact with eyes and skin. In case of skin contact, wash with soap and water. In case of eye contact, rinse immediately with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes and seek medical attention.
- Keep out of reach of children.
- If swallowed, do not induce vomiting. Contact a physician or poison control center immediately.
- Application tools should be cleaned with water immediately after use.
- Refer to the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for full hazard and first-aid information before use.
